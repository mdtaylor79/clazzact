The Artist

About

CLAZZ@CT is an artificial intelligence–created hip-hop artist developed by MD Taylor, founder and creator of Purpose Wave Entertainment. Inspired by Taylor's experiences mentoring young men, CLAZZ@CT was created to represent a thoughtful, positive young adult who sees beyond life's distractions and expresses what many young men think but may not always feel free to say.

His music blends sharp lyricism, clever wordplay, and authentic storytelling with a calm sense of curiosity. Rather than speaking at young listeners, CLAZZ@CT creates space for them to listen, reflect, question the world, and explore their own ideas without judgment or ridicule.

Each track presents realism through moments of triumph, struggle, growth, and personal exploration. His lyrics examine life with honesty and composure, offering perspective without pretending to have every answer. From introspective deep cuts to high-energy anthems, CLAZZ@CT delivers music with meaning—no filler, no filter.

Rooted in real experiences and driven by purpose, his verses are designed to linger long after the final bar, giving young men the freedom to think deeply, speak honestly, and find confidence in their own voice.
